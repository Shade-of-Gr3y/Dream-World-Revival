Pokémon GLOBAL LINK

When you participate in Random Matchup Rating Mode in Pokémon Black Version and Pokémon White Version, your battles will affect your Rating in the GBU and reflect how strong you are as a Trainer.
The GBU Ranking shows your battle records and compares them against the Ratings of Pokémon Trainers from around the world.
The top 100 Pokémon Trainers in each season's Overall Ranking receive special avatars for use in the PGL!

• You need to battle ten or more times in any battle format using the Random Matchup Rating Mode in order to be included in the Rankings for that season.
Note: Matches that end in a tie or are interrupted due to disconnection or other factors are not counted toward the number of battles required.
• Log in to the Pokémon Global Link to access the Global Battle Union. The “Details” button on the GBU home page takes you to your updated battle records.
*It may take a while for your data to be updated.

• Log in to the Pokémon Global Link to access the GBU. Click the “Ranking Season” button on the GBU home page to view the season’s Rankings.
*Rankings are updated weekly.
*Rankings are calculated based on your information at the time the Rankings are updated. You can check your current information by clicking "Details" on your Battle Records. Please note that Rankings shown might not reflect the latest Ratings.

• New seasons kick off every three months, with system maintenance scheduled before the starting and ending dates.
• After a season wraps up, the final battle results will be calculated for Trainers who participated in at least 10 battles during the season. The Rankings are scheduled to be announced two to three weeks later.

• Go to "Past Rankings" on the GBU home page to check Rankings from past seasons.

• For each season, the top 100 Pokémon Trainers in the Overall Ranking will receive trophy avatars.
• The trophy avatar appears in the selection of avatars on the Profile page of top-ranked players when the Ranking results are announced.

• Communication may be disrupted during battle if the Internet connection is unstable or if the battery of a Nintendo DS system is not sufficiently charged. For uninterrupted battles, please make sure your Internet connection is stable and your DS system is sufficiently charged. To provide reliable battle conditions for all, users who have frequent disconnections may be disqualified from GBU Rankings.

Pokémon Trainers from all over the world participate in the GBU, so the GBU's time standard is Coordinated Universal Time, known as UTC. Here are some comparisons, although regions that observe a daylight savings time may differ:

Seattle, Washington, U.S. time is UTC minus 8 hours
New York City, New York, U.S. time is UTC minus 5 hours
Ottawa, Ontario, Canada time is UTC minus 5 hours
London, England time is equal to UTC
Canberra, Australia time is UTC plus 10 hours

Examples:
11 a.m. EST (New York) is 6 a.m. UTC
11 a.m. EDT (New York) is 7 a.m. UTC

6 p.m. EST (Ottawa) is 1 p.m. UTC
6 p.m. EDT (Canberra) is 5 a.m. UTC (next day)
