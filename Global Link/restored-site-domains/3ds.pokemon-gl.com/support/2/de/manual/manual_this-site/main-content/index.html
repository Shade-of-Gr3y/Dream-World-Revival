About Your Profile

You can check your status in the Pokémon Global Link and your Game Card, and see information about your Game Pals and Dream Pals.

You can also switch and add Game Cards, change your avatar, and check your privacy settings.

PokéMileage Club

You can transfer the Poké Miles that you acquire in your Pokémon X or Pokémon Y game to your Pokémon Global Link account. Then, you can use them in the PokéMileage Club to play attractions or make purchases at the shop. You can buy things to help you on your adventure, like items and Berries, and send them to your game using Game Sync.

Note: PokéMileage Club attractions will be available at a later date.

Rating Battles

Select “Battle Spot” in the PSS (Player Search System) menu in Pokémon X or Pokémon Y to connect to the Internet and battle with other players from around the world. When playing Rating Battles online, you’ll receive a rank at the end of a season, based on your record of wins and losses. Seasons usually take place over a three-month period.

Each of the battle formats, such as Single, Double, or Triple Battles, has its own ranking.

Statistics about frequently used Pokémon will be displayed separately for the different battle formats.

Note: The PSS is a feature in Pokémon X and Pokémon Y that allows you to easily connect and communicate with other players at any time.

Online Competitions

Online Competitions for Pokémon X and Pokémon Y will be hosted from time to time. To participate, you’ll need to sign up for the competition in advance at the Pokémon Global Link. After the competition begins, participants can find a match against other participants at any time. A ranking based on each player’s rating will be finalized after the competition ends.

Note: More information about Online Competitions will be released at a later date.

News

If you change your Nintendo 3DS system, your friend list will change as well. If you use Game Sync after changing systems, information about your Friends will be updated to reflect the information on your new Nintendo 3DS system.

Also, if you change your Nintendo 3DS system and play in Rating Battles in any given Battle Format, your match history in that format will be reset and updated to reflect the information on your new Nintendo 3DS system. Please be careful when playing with your Game Card on a different Nintendo 3DS system than usual.

Note: If you perform a system transfer from your old Nintendo 3DS system to your new one, your data will transfer over and you won’t have to worry about these issues.

Events calendar

The events calendar contains info about special Pokémon distributions and competitions, and other news about Pokémon video games. Remember to check the calendar often to find out what’s going on!
