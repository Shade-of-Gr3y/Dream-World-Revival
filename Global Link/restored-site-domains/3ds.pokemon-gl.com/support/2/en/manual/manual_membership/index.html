What You Need

Game Sync ID

Registering the Game Sync ID code issued in your game lets you enjoy content that uses your game’s saved data in the PGL.

In the PokéMileage Club, you can use Poké Miles to get items that can be sent back to your game. In your profile, you can check your trade history at the Global Trade Station (GTS), as well as your battle history and past rankings in Rating Battles. You can also view photos that you took in game and check on your friend list.
