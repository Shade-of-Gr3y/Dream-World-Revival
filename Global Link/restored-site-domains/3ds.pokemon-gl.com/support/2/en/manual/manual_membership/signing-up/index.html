Signing Up

Introduction

Signing up for the Pokémon Trainer Club is the first step to playing at the Pokémon Global Link.

Note: Players in Japan or Korea, please select “Choose your region and language” at the home page of the PGL, and then choose the appropriate language and region.

About the Pokémon Trainer Club

The Pokémon Trainer Club is an official Pokémon fan club. There are no fees for registration or annual membership.
The Pokémon Trainer Club provides information about Pokémon and other fun features. Registering entitles you to play minigames, earn Trainer Tokens, and participate in exciting Pokémon-themed activities.

Registering for the Pokémon Trainer Club

If you don’t yet have an account at the Pokémon Trainer Club, click “SIGN UP!” on the PGL home page, and then follow the instructions. If you already have a Pokémon Trainer Club account, go ahead and enter your user name and password to log in at the Pokémon Global Link home page.

If you already have a Pokémon Trainer Club account, log in to your Trainer Club account from the Pokémon Global Link home page for English.

Next, sign up for the Pokémon Global Link account. This is a second account, separate from your Trainer Club account.

When you use the Pokémon Global Link

The following information from your Pokémon Trainer Club account will be used in the Pokémon Global Link:

・ Date of birth
・ Location by country (and state, if provided)

The Pokémon Company International may also use your registered name and email address to send you important notices related to the Pokémon Global Link.

Sign up for the Pokémon Global Link

In order to use the Pokémon Global Link with your Pokémon Trainer Club account, you need to register with the Game Sync ID generated from a North American or European region version of Pokémon X or Pokémon Y.

Note: You cannot register using the Game Sync ID generated by a copy of Pokémon X or Pokémon Y from another region.
Note: You will need a North American or European region Nintendo 3DS system (respectively) to play the North American or European region versions of the games.

If you change your Nintendo 3DS system, your friends list will change as well. If you use Game Sync after changing systems, information about your Friends will be updated to reflect the information on your new Nintendo 3DS system.

Also, if you change your Nintendo 3DS system and play in Rating Battles in any given Battle Format, your match history in that format will be reset and updated to reflect the information on your new Nintendo 3DS system. Please be careful when playing with your Game Card on a different Nintendo 3DS system than usual.

Note: If you perform a system transfer from your old Nintendo 3DS system to your new one, your data will transfer over and you won’t have to worry about these issues.

On the PSS menu (found on the lower screen) in your Pokémon X or Pokémon Y game, select “Game Sync” and connect to the Internet. Use the Game Sync ID Code displayed to register on the Pokémon Global Link.

Note: The PSS (Player Search System) is a feature in Pokémon X and Pokémon Y that allows you to easily connect and communicate with other players at any time.

Please note

・Only one Game Sync ID from Pokémon X and one Game Sync ID from Pokémon Y can be registered to any individual Pokémon Global Link account.

・You can’t register a Game Sync ID that has already been registered to another PGL account.

・Members logging in to the PGL with a Pokémon Trainer Club account must have a North American or European region version of Pokémon X or Pokémon Y. A North American or European region Nintendo 3DS system is also required to play the games.

・The Game Sync ID is a 16-character alphanumeric code. The numbers “0” and “1” and the letters “O” and “I” are not used in the codes. When entering your Game Sync ID, please be careful not to confuse the following characters: “8” and “B,” “Y” and “V,” “O” and “D,” and “A” and “R.”
