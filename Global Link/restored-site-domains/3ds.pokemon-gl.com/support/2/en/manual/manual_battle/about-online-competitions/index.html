About Online Competitions

Online Competitions can be signed up for in advance at the Pokémon Global Link. During the competition period, players battle one another by choosing Online Competition on the Battle Spot in the PSS menu in their games. By winning battles, players can improve their rating. At the end of each competition, a final ranking is determined.

Note: The first Online Competition is expected to be held in winter 2013. Further details will be announced at a later date.
