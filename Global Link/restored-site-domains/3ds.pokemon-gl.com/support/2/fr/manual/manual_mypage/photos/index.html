Photos

Info found here

You can share photos that Phil the Photo Guy took for you in the game by using Game Sync to send them to the PGL. You can view these photos in your profile.

At first, you can save only 12 game photos in the PGL. This number will increase as you gain Medals, to a maximum of 60 photos.

If you use Game Sync when you’ve already reached your maximum, any new photos that are uploaded will overwrite your saved photos, starting with the oldest one.

If you want to prevent a photo from being overwritten, you can select that photo and click the lock icon to lock it. When a photo is locked, it won’t be overwritten by newly uploaded photos. Instead, the next-oldest unlocked photo will be overwritten.

Note: You cannot lock every photo saved in the PGL. At least one photo must remain unlocked.

Phil the Photo Guy

At certain points in your Pokémon X or Pokémon Y game, you’ll have the ability to call Phil the Photo Guy over to take a picture of you. These photos can be saved.

Only the most recently taken photo will be uploaded to the Pokémon Global Link. Even if you take multiple photos in a row in your game, only the most recently taken photo will be uploaded.

Note: Photos taken by Phil the Photo Guy are saved to the SD Card in your Nintendo 3DS system. You can view any photos that aren’t uploaded to the Pokémon Global Link there.
